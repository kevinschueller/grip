# Grip

A twelve week, load based rehab program for medial elbow pain (golfer's elbow), delivered as a single page web app that lives on a phone home screen.

## Mode

Operate. The visitor opens it while sitting on the floor with a dumbbell beside them, usually one handed, because the other arm is the injured one. Every screen has to answer "what do I do right now, and did I already do it" in under two seconds.

The Learn tab is the one Read surface: it explains the mechanism and the rules, then gets out of the way.

## Who

One person: a lifter in his forties with a gradual onset medial elbow, no swelling, no numbness, pain provoked by gripping and by holding the arm straight. He trains, he works at a desk, he wants to be told what to do rather than handed a research digest. He will use this daily for twelve weeks and abandon it instantly if it feels like homework.

## The job

1. Show today's exact prescription, with doses that change by program week.
2. Let him stamp each item done, one handed, in one tap.
3. Track pain daily and show the trend over weeks, because the trend is the only honest measure of progress in tendinopathy.
4. Make the streak and the ledger pleasant enough that he opens it on day nine when nothing feels better yet.

## Truth boundaries

- This is education and a loading plan, not a diagnosis or medical advice.
- Loading principles follow the published approach shared by E3 Rehab, Physiotutors and Rehab Science: isometrics early, slow eccentric and heavy slow resistance later, pain held at or below 3 out of 10, no worsening the next morning.
- No invented clinical percentages, no fake benchmark numbers, no claims of a cure timeline beyond "most people improve over 6 to 12 weeks".
- Progress data never leaves the device. No account, no analytics, no server.

## Non goals

Social features, accounts, sync, push notifications, a coaching marketplace, or anything that asks for an email address.
