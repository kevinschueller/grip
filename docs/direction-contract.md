# Direction contract: Grip

Mode: Operate (Today, Program, Log), Read (Learn). Built at phone width first, 390 x 844, and it must hold at 360 and at 768.

## THESIS

A rehabilitation plan rendered as a machinist's job card: the session is a work order you stamp, the twelve weeks are a ruler, and progress is a bar being loaded. It refuses the health app default of a dark card grid with a glowing progress ring, and it refuses the editorial default of cream paper with a serif headline.

## OWN-WORLD

Three roles, no fourth: chalk ground, iron ink, oxide red field. Panels are 12px radius rectangles with a single hairline, never a border plus a wide shadow. Type is one family, Archivo, used at two widths: condensed 78 percent for display and figures, normal for reading. Labels are uppercase with wide tracking, never monospace. Icons are Phosphor regular, 1.5px effective stroke, tinted from ink.

## STORY

The visitor opens it, sees the week, the day type, and the exact doses. He taps a stamp per item. The tally fills. He logs pain on an eleven step scale and watches the line over weeks. The ledger and the loaded bar are the reward: no confetti, no cartoon mascot.

## FIRST VIEWPORT

A thin instrument header with the wordmark and a streak chip. Under it, a full width oxide red work order panel carrying WEEK 03 / 12, the day type in condensed 40px chalk, the item count and minutes, and a four segment tally. Under that, the session list on chalk, each row showing index, movement name, dose in tabular numerals, and a stamp control at the right edge inside thumb reach.

## FORM

Structure derived from a printed job card plus a steel rule. Signature interaction is the stamp: one tap fills the control, the row settles, and a completion stamp rotates onto the panel when the last item lands.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.
